**YAPCO** (*Yet Another Planner Creator*) turns a planner **template** into a complete, dated,
print-ready planner in **A4** (210 × 297 mm) or **A5** (148 × 210 mm). It lays out hundreds of
pages for two-sided printing, with mirrored margins for the binding, in Polish or English. You
print it blank on your own printer, fill it in by hand and keep it in a ring binder or a booklet.

![A day spread of the Balance edition, with the example filling](/docs/screens/day-balance.png)

## What you can do

- **Generate a whole planner.** Pick a template, edition, format, language, start date and
  length. YAPCO lays out every month, week and day: 500+ pages for six months.
- **Choose what goes in.** Editions are ready-made sets of *modules*; switch any module on or
  off for one planner.
- **Design visually.** A three-panel designer: the page structure, a real-size canvas with the
  binding margins, and a properties panel.
- **Work in two languages.** Every text has Polish and English side by side, including gendered
  Polish wording (*wdzięczny/a*).
- **Print at home.** PDF for printers with or without two-sided printing, A5 two per A4 sheet,
  an A5 booklet, a calibration sheet, and one month at a time.
- **Explain it.** Example filling in grey handwriting, and a printable guide to every page.
- **Keep it private.** Planners are stored only in your browser. No accounts, no server
  database, no analytics.
- **Use it offline.** Install it as an app; after the first visit it works without a connection.

## Templates

| Template | For |
| --- | --- |
| **Day by Day** (*Dzień po Dniu*) | A six-month day planner in three editions: **Basic**, **Balance** (wellbeing) and **Recovery Edition** (recovery from addiction). |
| **Week by Week** (*Tydzień po Tygodniu*) | A simple weekly planner for 1–12 months. |

See [Editions and modules](/docs/en/guides/editions/) for what each one contains.

## Two ways to use it

1. **In the browser**, on a site that already runs YAPCO (for example your own
   `planner.example.com`). Nothing to install: go to [Your first planner](/docs/en/start/quick-start/).
2. **On your own computer or server.** YAPCO is open source (MIT). See
   [Run it on your computer](/docs/en/start/run-locally/) or
   [Self-hosting](/docs/en/self-hosting/overview/).
