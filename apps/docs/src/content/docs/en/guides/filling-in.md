## The printable guide

**Guide: how to fill in the planner** (the link on the dashboard, and in Export) explains every
page of the chosen edition, with a filled-in example next to each. It is A4 and printable: print
it once and keep it at the front of the binder, or give it to someone starting their planner.

![The guide](/docs/screens/guide.png)

The guide follows the edition: the Basic guide shows Basic pages, the Recovery Edition guide
explains HALT-B, triggers and the crisis plan.

## Example filling

Switch on **Example filling** in the Preview or in Export to print grey handwritten entries and
short notes on every page. It is meant for showing someone how to use the planner; leave it off
for the planner you will write in.

## A few tips

- **Keep it short.** A few words per box is enough. An empty box on a hard day is fine too.
- **Mornings: three priorities.** Not a to-do list; the three things that make the day good.
- **Evenings: one honest sentence.** What happened, what helped, what you are grateful for.
- **Each week and month, look back.** The reviews only ask about what the days collected.
- **Recovery Edition:** fill in the crisis section with your therapist, while you are calm; it is
  written for the moment when you are not.
