Everything is printed from **Export** (the button at the top right).

![The export screen](/docs/screens/export.png)

## 1. What to print

Tick the parts: the introduction, each month, the crisis section. **Every part fills whole
sheets**, so for a ring binder you can print one month at a time and leave out the introduction
once you have it. *Months only* and *All* select quickly.

## 2. How you print

| Option | For |
| --- | --- |
| **Two-sided, automatic** | A printer that prints both sides itself (duplex). Flip on the long edge. |
| **Two-sided, by hand** | A printer without duplex: you get a *fronts* file and a *backs* file. |
| **A5 on A4 paper (two per sheet)** | An A5 planner on ordinary A4 paper; cut each stack once down the middle. |
| **A5 paper** | A printer with an A5 tray. |
| **A5 booklet on A4 paper** | Folded bundles to sew or staple: see [Ring binder or booklet](/docs/en/guides/binding/). |

Also: **Page numbers**, the **YAPCO apple beside the page number**, and **Example filling** (leave
it off for the planner you will write in).

## 3. Create the PDF

**Create PDF**. The file downloads to your *Downloads* folder, named after the planner and the
part, for example `day-by-day-month-2026-11.pdf`. Large parts are made in pieces and joined;
the progress shows *Rendering part 2 of 5…*.

## 4. Print

- In the print dialog choose **Actual size** / **100 %**. Never *Fit to page*: the margins and
  sizes are exact.
- Two-sided: flip on the **long edge** for A4, on the **short edge** for A5 two per sheet and for
  booklets.
- By hand: print the fronts, put the stack back as the calibration sheet showed, print the backs.

## The calibration sheet

The first time with a printer, click **Download calibration sheet** and print it on both sides:

- both rulers must measure exactly **100 mm**;
- the crosses on the two sides should line up when you hold the sheet against the light;
- for printing by hand, it shows whether you need **Backs in reverse order**.

## Without the export service

**Print from the browser instead** works offline and without the export service, but only in
reading order: no two-per-sheet and no by-hand options. Choose *Save as PDF* in the dialog to
keep a file.
