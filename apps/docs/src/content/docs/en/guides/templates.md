A **template** is the recipe for a planner: its sections, the page layouts, the blocks on each
page and all texts in Polish and English. When you create a planner, YAPCO expands the template
for your dates into real pages.

## Day by Day (*Dzień po Dniu*)

A day planner for six months (1–12 possible), with a two-page spread for every day, a week
spread and review each week, and an opening and a review each month. It has three
[editions](/docs/en/guides/editions/) and eight [modules](/docs/en/guides/modules/), example
filling on every page, and a printable guide.

![A day spread of the Recovery Edition](/docs/screens/day-recovery.png)

## Week by Week (*Tydzień po Tygodniu*)

A simple weekly planner for 1–12 months (a year by default):

| Section | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| Introduction | A cover and "My year": goals, what to remember, notes |
| Each month | A month spread: the calendar and goals on the left; important dates, a to-do list and notes on the right |
| Each week | A week spread: Monday–Wednesday with three priorities and "Remember"; Thursday–Sunday with a to-do list and notes |
| End of each month | A notes page with a 5 mm dot grid |

A week belongs to the month its Monday falls in. It has no modules, example filling or guide
yet.

## Your own template

Change a planner in the designer until you like it, then **Export → Save template as JSON**.
The file holds the layout only: no names, dates or your notes. Importing it (**Import JSON…** on
the dashboard) creates a fresh planner from it; you can share the file with others.

To write a new template in code, see [Writing a template](/docs/en/developers/templates/).
