## Preview

**Preview** shows the whole planner as it will print: spreads or single pages, at real size.

- **Example filling** shows grey handwritten entries on every page, to see how a page is meant
  to be used.
- **Binding & margin guides** show where the rings or the fold will be.
- **Print view** opens the pages as the browser would print them.
- The planner settings: **Start date**, **Length** and **modules**, then **Regenerate**. Edited
  pages that no longer fit the new dates are listed.

Blank pages marked *blank (alignment)* are added on purpose, so that sections start on the right
side of a spread.

## Content

**Content** holds the planner's libraries: **Quotes**, **Affirmations**, **Prompts**, **SOS
steps**, **Warning signs** and **Exercises**.

- Edit each entry in Polish and English side by side; search and filter by category.
- **Only items with problems** lists missing translations, texts too long for their box,
  duplicates, and quotes that need an author or source.
- **Import CSV** / **Export CSV** to edit in Excel or Google Sheets.
- **Re-deal quotes** so every day gets one; with fewer than 30 quotes some repeat within a month.

Only add quotes you are allowed to share: your own words, the public domain, or with the licence
and source filled in.

## Translations

**Translations** lists every text of the planner in both languages, grouped: the planner,
page templates, section labels, texts in blocks, variables and the content library.

- **Show only missing translations** filters to what still needs work.
- **Copy EN → PL** / **Copy PL → EN** fills one side from the other as a starting point.

The **Export** screen warns when texts are not translated into the planner's language yet.

## Polish gender forms

Polish texts that depend on gender are written once for both: *zauważyłeś/aś*, *wdzięczny/a*,
*chciał(a)bym*. In templates they are `{g:masculine|feminine}` tokens, for example
`{g:gotowy|gotowa}`.
