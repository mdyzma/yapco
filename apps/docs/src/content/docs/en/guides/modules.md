A **module** is a part of the template that can be switched on or off: whole pages, single
blocks, or the wording of a block. An **edition** is just a ready-made choice of modules.

Choose modules when you create a planner (**Modules** under *Edition* in the New planner form),
or later in the **Preview**'s planner settings. When your choice matches no edition, the edition
shows as *Custom*.

## The modules of "Day by Day"

| Module | On in | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A good start** (*Na dobry początek*) | Balance | Six front pages: an agreement with yourself, a good life, more / less, values, strengths, what recharges you. |
| **Recovery and sobriety** (*Zdrowienie i trzeźwość*) | Recovery | The sobriety day counter, craving and tension, triggers, what protected me, meeting markers, the if–then plan, the contract and safety rules, "My patterns", the crisis section, and recovery wording throughout. |
| **HALT-B check** (*Skala HALT-B*) | Recovery | Hungry, Angry, Lonely, Tired, Bored: on the day page and in "My week". |
| **Wellbeing** (*Dobrostan*) | Balance | "A good life" on the evening page, the value of the month and "My month in practice". |
| **Situation analysis (CBT)** (*Analiza sytuacji*) | — | A weekly page: situation, thoughts, feelings, body, behaviour, another way to see it. |
| **Day+** (*Dzień+*) | — | The rest of "My 24 hours", plus one important and one pleasant thing each day, in place of the plan of the day. |
| **Mindfulness** (*Uważność*) | — | Four short practices at the front, and "My mindfulness practice" after each "My week". |
| **Productivity** (*Produktywność*) | — | A "My projects" spread each month, and a focus block and "Not doing this week" on the week spread. |

With neither *Recovery* nor *Wellbeing* on, the planner is **Basic**: it gets its own simpler
evening page, "My week" and "My month".

## What happens when you switch a module

- Pages of a module appear or disappear everywhere they repeat (every day, week or month).
- Blocks that depend on it appear or disappear on their pages.
- Blocks with different wording per module (for example "protect my sobriety" and "take care of
  myself") change their text.
- Your own edits to pages that stay are kept.

The **Week by Week** template has no modules.
