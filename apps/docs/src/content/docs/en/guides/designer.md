The **Design** screen has three panels: the structure and components on the left, the page at
real size in the middle, and the properties of what you select on the right.

![The designer](/docs/screens/designer.png)

## The left panel

| Tab | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Pages** | The planner's structure: sections, and what repeats each month, week and day. Switch an entry off to leave it out of every month, or drag it to reorder. |
| **Layers** | The blocks of the current page, in groups. Drag to reorder within a group. |
| **Components** | Blocks you can drag onto the page: text, writing lines, dot grid, lists, scales, calendar, HALT-B, Wheel of Life, contacts, images… |
| **Templates** | The page layouts of the template; *Show first page* jumps to one. |
| **Content** | Shortcut to quotes and prompts. |
| **Variables** | Values printed on the pages, such as your name; those filled in automatically are listed separately. |

## One page or all of them

At the top, **Apply changes to** decides what an edit changes:

- **All pages using this layout** (the default): change every day page at once, for example.
- **Only this page**: change one date only. Such blocks are marked *Changed on this page only*,
  and **Reset this page** removes those changes.

Your edits survive regenerating the planner for new dates.

## Blocks

Click a block to select it. In the properties panel you can:

- edit its texts in **both languages** side by side, and *Copy PL → EN* when a text is missing;
- change the **typography** (font, size, weight, line height, alignment, colour);
- change the **layout**: height (fit content, fixed mm, a share of the free space), padding,
  border, corner radius;
- choose where it **prints**: every page, left-hand pages only or right-hand pages only;
- **Duplicate**, **Move up / down**, **Lock**, **Hide on this page** or **Delete** it.

Blocks with different wording per module show a **Module wording** group.

## The canvas

- **View**: a spread or one page; zoom and *Fit*.
- **Guides** show the binding margin (inner) and the outer margin; the **5 mm grid** and
  **Snap** help to place blocks.
- **Format** and **Planner language** switch the whole planner; your edits carry over.
- **Undo** and **Redo** (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y) cover every change.

The page number box lets you jump to any page (*Go to page*).
