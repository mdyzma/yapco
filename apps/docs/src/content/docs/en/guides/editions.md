"Day by Day" comes in three editions that scale from the simplest to the most structured. Each
is a ready-made set of [modules](/docs/en/guides/modules/), so any element can still be switched
on or off for one planner.

| Edition | Polish | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Basic** | Podstawowy | The simplest planner: priorities and a plan for the day, an evening page of dots with gratitude, a short review each week and month. |
| **Balance** | Balans | Everyday life and wellbeing, with some structure: a check-in and check-out, the evening reflection, "A good life", the Wheel of Life. No addiction or therapy wording. |
| **Recovery Edition** | Terapeutyczny | Recovery from addiction: sobriety days, craving, HALT-B, triggers, the contract and the crisis section; the wellbeing parts are optional. |

Six months come to about 483 pages as Basic, 501 as Balance and 541 as the Recovery Edition (A4).

## Choosing

- Start with **Basic** if you have never kept a planner, or want a light daily habit.
- **Balance** suits a planner for wellbeing, habits and reflection.
- The **Recovery Edition** is made to be used alongside addiction therapy; the crisis section
  is best filled in with a therapist.

You can change the edition's modules later, in the preview's planner settings; existing pages
and your edits are kept.
